Privacy Policy — Boot Chain

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Boot Chain is a reference app for Android boot chain and partition layout information. This privacy policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data collection

Boot Chain does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, and does not include advertising.

Permissions

Boot Chain does not request any device permissions.

Third parties

Boot Chain does not share any data with third parties, because no data is collected.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via the app's GitHub repository: github.com/skulldreamz1337/Boot-Chain
